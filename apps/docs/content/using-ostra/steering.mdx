title: "Steering"
description: "Send the agent a message while it works."

---

The task's driver steers it: one member at a time, so two people never prompt the same agent at once. Everyone else who can open the project watches, and can **Take over** in one action.

## Send a message

Type in the task's composer, then:

- **Send.** For an agent that takes a message mid-turn, as OpenCode does, the message reaches the model at the agent's next call, after any tool step that's running. For an agent that doesn't, the message is queued: everyone sees it in the thread as "queued", and it's delivered when the turn ends. When the session is idle, **Send** starts the next turn.
- **Interrupt and send.** Stops the current turn and sends the message as the next prompt, on every agent. It's labelled that way in the composer while a turn runs.

A message can carry files: paste them, drop them on the page, or pick them with the paperclip. Your message shows as a right-aligned bubble with your name and the time over it, and its files above it. See [Attaching files](/docs/using-ostra/files).

When someone else is driving, the composer says so: take over first to send.

## Answer a question

An agent's question waits in the thread as an amber block, in the place of the step it holds back, and the task's state names it: `Needs you · question`. Its fields come as choices, each a radio row, and a field that asks for text gets a field of its own.

On a question with choices, the choices end with **Something else…**, which opens a field for your own words and puts the focus in it. It's there when the agent takes an answer in words, as OpenCode does; an agent that can't takes one of its choices only. Picking a choice doesn't send on its own: **Send answer** wakes once every field holds a value, and `⌘↵` or `Ctrl+↵` sends too.

The driver answers. Everyone else sees the same block quiet, at half strength, with **Take over to answer** and who is driving.

Once answered, the block becomes a receipt in its place: `Sam answered “Use a queue”`, or the choice's label, with its time. Your own words reach the agent as they are, and its next message shows it got them.

When the block is scrolled out of view, a pill sits on a band above the composer: `Needs you · question`, or `Needs you · plan` or `Needs you · approval` for what waits. Press it to bring the block back into view and put the focus in it; the pill goes once the block is in view.

A question nobody answers in time is cancelled, and the thread says so. See [Approvals](/docs/using-ostra/approvals) for what waits as an approval or a plan.

## Stop

**Stop** asks the agent to stop. The task's work is saved, as after every turn: the branches, the unfinished work in a hidden ref, and the agent's session. You can [reopen](/docs/using-ostra/reopening) it later.

ostra also stops a session by itself, and the task shows **Needs you**:

- after its maximum time, 2 hours by default (`OSTRA_SESSION_MAX_MINUTES`);
- when it stalls, with no agent event for 10 minutes (`OSTRA_SESSION_STALL_MINUTES`);
- at the task's [spend cap](/docs/using-ostra/budgets).
