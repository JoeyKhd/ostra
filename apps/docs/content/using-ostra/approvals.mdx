## What needs you

A task waits on a person for one of these, and shows **Needs you** in amber:

- **an approval:** the agent wants to do something one of its profile's [approval rules](/docs/agents-and-models/approval-rules) holds back;
- **a question:** the agent asks something it needs to know;
- **a plan:** with [plan first](/docs/agents-and-models/plan-first), the plan to approve;
- **a draft:** a refine task's draft of the ticket;
- **a session that ended without changes**, or files the agent left outside every repo;
- **a pull request** ready for your review;
- **a limit:** the session stalled, ran for its maximum time, or the task reached its spend cap.

They're on your home screen first, and on the **Needs you** page. They also reach you as notifications, in the panel, as a comment on the ticket, or by email, as you choose in your notification settings.

## Approve or refuse

The approval says what the agent asks to do: the file it wants to edit, the command, the address or the tool. **Any member of the project can answer. The first answer wins.**

- **Approve once.**
- **Approve for this task:** also approves later requests on this task that match the narrow rule the panel shows.
- **Deny**, or **Deny and send the reason:** denying ends the agent's turn, and it's prompted again with your reason. The other open approvals close as cancelled.

Every answer is in the audit log, and the task's thread shows who answered.

A question is answered by the driver: **Take over to answer** when someone else is driving, then **Send answer**. See [Answer a question](/docs/using-ostra/steering#answer-a-question).
