## How a phone reaches the bookkeeping

There's no hover on a phone, so nothing lives only in the tooltip:

1. **Tap a run's line:** its footer holds the turn's numbers (`Turn 1 · High · 41.2k in · 6.3k out · 2.1k reasoning · $0.62 so far`) and `Event log ›`, which opens the Event log tab at the turn's first event (`s6-phone-open.webp`).
2. **`…` → Properties:** the panel as a bottom sheet (milestone 8), with Usage and Session (saved work, box).
3. **`…` → Event log:** every event, as on desktop.

The turn's time on its first line is still there, but on a phone it isn't a button: the footer is the way in.

### Differences from desktop

- **Bubbles** `max-width: 88%`, 15px. **Prose** 15px.
- **A run's line** wraps (`white-space: normal`), 14.5px/1.45, and its tap target is the whole line, at least 44px tall. The chevron and the time follow the last word.
- **An opened run:** the verb sits inline before its subject (`22px icon | verb | subject`, 8px gaps), and a long subject wraps under itself; rows at least 36px. A thought's excerpt stays one line with `show ›` at its end. The footer wraps by fact (no fact is split) and `Event log ›` takes its own 28px line.
- **The question:** choices 44px tall, 15px; `Send answer` full width, 44px, with the footnote under it.
- **The pill** as desktop, centred in a 46px solid band above the dock.
- **The composer:** a 50px box, radius 14px; the paperclip 44×42; the field 15.5px; Send 40px, radius 10px. Under it, 12px: the hint (`Reaches OpenCode after your answer.`) and the next turn's model, short (`Sonnet 5.5 · High ⌄`). `Stop` and `Take it local` are in the `…` menu (milestone 2).
