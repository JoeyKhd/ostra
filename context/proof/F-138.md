## Notes

- 2026-11-20, the subagent turn fix (ADR 0067), before ticket 39: on the pinned OpenCode against the stub model, a one-shot task whose agent starts a subagent and ends its turn keeps its agent until the subagent reports and the woken agent answers; the subagent's work and that answer are the turn's events, before `turn.ended`, and the session ends after it. Partial because the subagent's own reporting reaches the thread flat, under the main agent's turn: the window that shows it on its own is ticket 39's. The capability ticket 39 names exists as `AgentCapabilities.subagents` (`background` or `none`); the row about it waits for the flat-calls case on the page.
