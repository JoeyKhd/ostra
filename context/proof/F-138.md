## Notes

- 2026-11-20, the subagent turn fix (ADR 0067), before ticket 39: on the pinned OpenCode against the stub model, a one-shot task whose agent starts a subagent and ends its turn keeps its agent until the subagent reports and the woken agent answers; the subagent's work and that answer are the turn's events, before `turn.ended`, and the session ends after it.
- 2026-11-20, milestone 7's ticket 39: protocol minor 6 carries a subagent's start and states (`subagent.started`, `subagent.status`) and the subagent on `agent.message`, `agent.thought`, `tool.called`, `plan.updated` and `approval.requested`; the driver names each child's updates, states and approvals from OpenCode's `opencode/session/child_update` and `_meta["opencode/child-session"]`, and joins a child to its starting call through `rawOutput.metadata.sessionID`; the fold (`packages/task-view`) builds each turn's tree from the stored events; the page draws the badge and the window. The agent contract suite runs two parity cases on every adapter ("F-138: a subagent's start, its state, its calls and its answer are named as its own, with subagents background" and its `none` counterpart, in `packages/adapter-sdk/src/testing/agent-suite.ts`), OpenCode's on the pinned binary against the stub model.
- **Tokens and spend per subagent** wait for an ADR on attributing calls at the key proxy: nothing in ACP gives them (`research/opencode-subagents.md`, item 5). Until then a node shows no tokens line, which the window's e2e test checks, and the task's own figures count every call (F-089, F-084).
- **The recorded log** (`packages/adapters/adapter-opencode/evidence/opencode-subagents-acp.txt`) is replayed to the real `OpenCodeAdapter` and driver through `packages/task-view/src/testing/recorded-subagents.ts`. It's the cleaned log: a child's calls carry no kind or input there, so they fold as calls of kind `other`, and run F's nested `subagent` call has no output to join, so the nested subagent is proved on the scripted fake that plays OpenCode at `experimental.subagent_depth` 2 (`[[fake:subagent-tree]]`) and in the fold's own test.
- **The browser checks** run by hand (`.github/workflows/proof.yml`): 5 tests at desktop and at phone width, on the seeded tasks of `apps/web/e2e/seed-subagents.ts`. The final screenshots compared with the targets, in both themes and at 390px, were kept outside the repo, as `m7-subagents-*-final.png`.
