## Notes

- **Ticket 14 (2026-11-20)** built the outside start (`apps/core/src/starts/outside.ts`: `startFromTrackerInTx`, `matchStarterInTx`), the answers (`packages/domain/src/use-cases/outside-tasks.ts`: `approveOutsideTask`, `rejectOutsideTask`, `setOutsideApproval`) and the `poolOnly` rule in admission (`goesToPersonalRunner`, `poolQueueInTx`, `dispatchTask`). Every case runs the real start, the real answers and the real admission against Postgres: an unmatched starter's task is `awaiting_approval` with no prompt, no dispatch and no ticket write owed, invisible to a pool's queue, with a needs-you item for the project's members and a `task_approval` notification for each of them; it runs the project's default code profile; after a member's approval it waits for a pool even with the approver's personal runner online, and a pool runner takes it; a row that names a member and their personal runner stays on pools; a member without access to a restricted project gets `not-found`, as does another organization's admin; of an approve and a reject sent at once, one wins and the other gets `not-awaiting-approval`; the switch is on for a new project, a project admin's and a member's change is `FORBIDDEN`, and an organization admin's is an audit entry.
- **Why `partial`:** the "needs approval" list a member sees is the API's (`tasks.needsApproval`, ticket 26) and the panel's (ticket 33); the switch's control is ticket 26's `projects.setOutsideApproval`. Their tests finish both rows. The live step is milestone 6's demo, step 10 (ticket 39).
- **Break-it:** dropping `poolOnly` from `dispatchTask`'s runner choice (`personalRunnerId` and `startedByMember`) made "admission never offers a poolOnly task to a personal runner" and "with the switch off an outside task starts at once" fail: the personal runner got the lease. Restored.
- **Ticket 07 (2026-11-20)**, Linear's adapter: `getUser` reads `user(id)` for the starter match: the address when the token can read it, and when Linear refuses the field the rest is read without it, so a person with no readable address is matched as outside. An unknown ID is `not-found`; the address never reaches a log line.
- **Ticket 26 (2026-11-20)** added the API: `tasks.needsApproval({ projectId | null })` (every task waiting for approval in the projects the member can open: who started it by the tracker's name, never an address or the tracker's ID, its ticket, the profile it runs and the project's pools, plus the project's switch and who last changed it), `tasks.approve` and `tasks.reject` (any member who can open the project, through ticket 14's use cases), and `projects.setOutsideApproval` (an organization admin's, on the procedure and in the use case). The second row is proved by it. The first stays `partial` until the panel's Needs approval list (ticket 33).
- **Ticket 33 (2026-11-20)** put the list on the screen: a project's Needs approval (`/projects/[project-id]/needs-approval`, `apps/web/src/components/approvals/`), oldest first, one task open with who started it in Linear, its ticket, its profile and its pool, and "Approve" or "Reject" with a reason; its count in the sidebar; Home's row, whose "Approve" opens it; and the task page's banner with "Started in Linear by". The panel's switch is an organization admin's, read-only to everyone else. Proved end to end in `apps/web/e2e/20-agent-mode.spec.ts` (an approve, a reject with a reason, both standing after a reload, the tasks moved on); the first row is `proved`. The live run is milestone 6's demo, step 10 (ticket 39).
