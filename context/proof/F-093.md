Notes (2026-11-19, milestone 5's ticket 02): the executor contract gains the capability `nodeWait` and `create`'s `onWaiting`, which hears `waiting { reason: "node" | "image-pull", since }` and then `scheduled { afterMs }`. No row is proved yet: the `kubernetes` executor is ticket 07 and the runner's `box.waiting` is ticket 20. The suite's case "F-093: a box that waits for a node says so, then that it was scheduled, before it's ready; an executor that never waits says nothing" is what `kubernetes` passes against ticket 06's fake API; it failed on the tiny executor's fleet variant when `scheduled` was never said. The suite's cases live in `packages/adapter-sdk/src/testing/executor-suite.ts`, which isn't a test file, so they are named here rather than cited: `defineExecutorContractSuite` runs them in CI from `packages/adapter-sdk/src/contract-suites.test.ts` (the tiny executor and its fleet variant), `packages/adapters/adapter-executor-host/src/host-executor.contract.test.ts` and `packages/adapters/adapter-executor-docker/src/docker-executor.contract.test.ts`.

Notes (2026-11-19, milestone 5's ticket 06): the fake Kubernetes API (`packages/adapters/adapter-executor-kubernetes/src/testing/fake-api.ts`) is what ticket 07's executor and ticket 20's runner are tested against; it proves no row by itself. Its own tests (`fake-api.test.ts`) show `@kubernetes/client-node` creating, watching and deleting a pod and its owned secret, the unschedulable switch's `TriggeredScaleUp`, a native sidecar refused at 1.28, and the process mode running two containers that talk on loopback and ending both on delete; each failed when its part of the fake was broken (the 1.28 refusal, the scale-up event, the garbage collector, the kill on delete).

Notes (2026-11-19, milestone 5's ticket 07): `KubernetesExecutor` (`packages/adapters/adapter-executor-kubernetes/src/kubernetes-executor.ts`) makes a box's pod (`pod.ts`, built as typed values) and its secrets through the `KubeApi` port (`kube-api.ts`, over `@kubernetes/client-node`), against ticket 06's fake API over HTTPS. The executor contract suite runs against it in `packages/adapters/adapter-executor-kubernetes/src/kubernetes-executor.contract.test.ts`, on the fake cluster's process mode: the pod's real init runs as a local process from the pod's own spec and secret, and the test plays the pod's sidecar through the bridge, so every case of the suite (the task token's, the size's, the registry image's, the node wait's and release's included) runs on a pod. The rows stay `partial`: the services are ticket 08, the runner's dispatch accepted at once and its `box.waiting` are ticket 20, the chart's line on 1.29 is ticket 23 (the configuration reference has it, under `OSTRA_K8S_NAMESPACE`), and a real cluster (kind 1.29 and the current release, a box ready in under 60 s) is ticket 35's.

Notes (2026-11-19, milestone 5's ticket 08): the services are native sidecars after the sidecar, in the environment's order, and `prepare` reports their health. The wait reads the pod's `PodScheduled` condition and its events through the port: `Unschedulable` with the autoscaler's `TriggeredScaleUp` is `waiting: node` until it's scheduled, then `scheduled` with how long it took; past `OSTRA_K8S_NODE_WAIT_MINUTES` the box fails as `no-node` (`failureOf`), which ticket 13 requeues; `Unschedulable` with no scale-up fails as `no-room` after a 30 s grace (an autoscaler posts its event seconds after the scheduler's); a pull still under way 10 s after scheduling is `waiting: image-pull`. These run on the port's in-memory double with a clock the test moves (`node-wait.test.ts`), and the scale-up wait against the fake API in `kubernetes-executor.test.ts`. The break-it the ticket names, removing the scale-up check, failed the waiting test.
