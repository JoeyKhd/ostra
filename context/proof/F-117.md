Notes (2026-11-20, milestone 5's ticket 34): the harness is `e2e/load/` (`pnpm load:run`). Its simulated runners are `apps/runner`'s own peer and disk spool with a scripted agent in place of OpenCode, joined with a real join token and approved through `packages/api`; tasks are started, admitted by the real queue, dispatched by the real dispatcher, and steered through `packages/api` on three core replicas and one Postgres. The record of this Windows machine is `context/specs/m5-the-fleet/demo/load-windows.md`: it proves the harness and gives a number, not F-117's bar, which is 1,000 tasks on three replicas and a Postgres of its own on a Linux server of 16 or more vCPUs (ADR 0061, ticket 38). So the load row stays `partial` and F-117 stays `building`. The first row is `partial`: the runs show three `core` replicas and one `web` over one Postgres, with steers routed by Postgres to whichever replica holds the runner, but not several `web` replicas or `all`. The gate row waits for milestone 7's run on the release candidate (ADR 0064).
