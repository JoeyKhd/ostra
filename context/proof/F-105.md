## Notes

- **Ticket 08 (2026-11-20)**, GitHub's adapter: `listReviewComments` reads one review's line comments, each with its path, line, side, body and author, for the prompt core builds when a member requests changes. GitHub's `GET /pulls/{n}/reviews/{id}/comments` has no `line` or `side` (seen live on `test1`, F-100's notes), so it reads the PR's review comments and keeps that review's. Milestone 4's `listReviews` and review webhooks give the review itself. The row stays `partial`: the reopen and the prompt are core's (ticket 20).
- **Ticket 20 (2026-11-20)**, core: milestone 4's `pull_request_review` handling enqueues `changes.reopen` (keyed by the review) for a `submitted` `changes_requested` review whose author is a person (never a bot or an App) linked to an active member who can open the project; anyone else's is recorded as ignored with the reason, and nothing is posted on GitHub. The job (`reopenOnChangesRequested`, `packages/domain/src/review-loop/changes-requested.ts`) reads the review on the host again (`listReviews`, which now carries the body and page, additively), its line comments (`listReviewComments`), checks the reviewer again, and sends one member message from the reviewer with intent `changes-requested` (the domain's `changesRequestedPrompt`, the text as marked data blocks) through `sendMemberMessageInTx`: a steer after the running turn, or a reopen through the budget gate (origin `changes-requested`) and the spend cap. A refused send keeps nothing; a task between sessions retries. It writes a `TaskRound` (`changes_requested`, never automatic), `round.started`, and `pr.comment_received` with the review's page. The task keeps its driver and no `TaskCoAuthor` row is written for the reviewer (ADR 0040, amended).
