**The idle row, on `host`.** Milestone 1's final demo (2026-11-19) ran on a stack with `OSTRA_BOX_IDLE_MINUTES=1`, on a runner with one slot. In both runs, each task's session ended about a minute after its turn, its agent's process tree stopped, and the next task took the freed slot 4 s later (core's `task.session-ended` and `dispatch.offered` lines in the two logs). After the run, no agent process was left below the runner, and the three task folders left were all of `stopped` boxes. An earlier run on the default 15 minutes, whose log was lost to a `pnpm dev:reset`, showed the same at 15 minutes: the runner logged `the session was idle for its limit: ending it ... idleMinutes=15` and took the next task, which had waited from 05:41:31 to 05:56:31 UTC (`demo.md`, Q268). The `docker` and `kubernetes` cases wait for the Linux run.
