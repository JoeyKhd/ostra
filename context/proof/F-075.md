## Notes

- **The sweep** (ticket 19) runs in its own Playwright project, `phone-sweep`: Chromium as a touch phone (Pixel 7's profile) at 390 × 844. It seeds its own admin and world (`e2e/seed-routes-world.ts`), so it needs no other spec: `pnpm --filter @ostra/web exec playwright test --project phone-sweep`, after `next build`. Every `page.tsx` of `src/app` must be in `e2e/routes.ts`, or the naming check fails. Signed-out pages (sign-in, reset, an invitation, the old setup page) are swept signed out.
- **What it checks:** the document never scrolls sideways (a table or a diff scrolls inside its own box, which the check allows); every button, link drawn as a button, field, select, tab and combobox is at least 44 px tall on a coarse pointer (a checkbox, a switch or a radio is pressed through its row). The touch size is one rule in `globals.css` under `@media (pointer: coarse)`, so a mouse keeps the dense desktop sizes.
- **The "needs you" moments by tap:** an approval answered with "Approve once" on Needs you leaves the task `running`; a question answered there queues the task's next turn (no runner holds the seeded session); a reopen from the task's composer, reached from its Needs you row, leaves it `queued`. Done lists both answers with who gave them.
- **The navigation drawer** (below 1024 px the sidebar opens as a drawer): an admin's rows are taller than a small phone's screen, so the drawer scrolls inside itself, as the sidebar does, and its last row, the account menu with Sign out, is in reach by tap at 320 × 568. Before it scrolled, the account menu sat below the screen on a phone, and the phone runs of `01-shell` and `17-theme` failed there once the e2e organization had many projects.
- **The other two sentences** say what F-075 is not (an app) and how it was made (shadcn/ui, desktop first). No test can fail on them; they stay `missing` until ticket 32 records how the ledger proves a statement of scope, and the live check is Part A step 7 of the demo (spec section 3).
