# F-120: Snapshot registry

The proof of F-120's "done when" in `context/features.md`: one row per condition, quoted verbatim. The rules are in `context/proof/README.md`, and `pnpm check:proof` checks this file.

| Condition | Proved by | Kind | Break-it | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| An admin sets a container registry the organization owns (such as GHCR, ECR or Harbor). | — | — | — | missing |
| Runners push snapshots to it, and `docker` hosts and `kubernetes` pools pull them from it. | — | — | — | missing |
| A `docker` host with no registry set builds and caches the snapshot itself. | — | — | — | missing |
| A `kubernetes` pool refuses to start without a registry, and its snapshots are built by a rootless BuildKit pod (ADR 0038, Q95, round 14). | — | — | — | missing |

Notes (2026-11-19, milestone 5's ticket 02): the executor contract gains the capability `registry` (`required`, `optional`, `none`), `create`'s `image` (by digest, with its pull credential) and `buildSnapshot`'s `push` and `digest`. No row is proved yet: the pushes and pulls themselves are tickets 09 (`docker`), 07 and 21 (`kubernetes`). The suite's case "F-120: a box from a registry image is pulled by digest with its credential, which is in no container's environment, files or process list; with no registry an image is refused" is what each of them passes: it failed when the tiny executor's fleet variant pulled by tag, when the pull token was written into the box's files, and when `refuseUnfitBox` let an image through on `registry: "none"`. Until ticket 09, `docker` refuses a build asked to push (`packages/adapters/adapter-executor-docker/src/docker-snapshot-box.test.ts` > "F-120: until docker pushes (ticket 09), a build asked to push to a registry is refused, and nothing is built"). The suite's cases live in `packages/adapter-sdk/src/testing/executor-suite.ts`, which isn't a test file, so they are named here rather than cited: `defineExecutorContractSuite` runs them in CI from `packages/adapter-sdk/src/contract-suites.test.ts` (the tiny executor and its fleet variant), `packages/adapters/adapter-executor-host/src/host-executor.contract.test.ts` and `packages/adapters/adapter-executor-docker/src/docker-executor.contract.test.ts`.
