## Notes

- 2026-11-19, ticket 10 (m3): the second row is `partial`. Its Linear half is proved: `findUsersByEmail` matches a member's address to an active Linear user, and the mention it returns, posted in a comment, is a mention in Linear (checked live: Linear's `bodyData` holds the user-mention node). Ticket 11 proves the panel's and the ticket's channels on their own (the panel on in-memory rows, the tracker on the fake Linear and the Jira fake: one comment per key, mentions, an edit in place, a title that can't make markup). The fan-out (ticket 20) and email (tickets 12, 13, 21) prove the rest. Linear doesn't notify a person about their own mention, so the live run, made with the maintainer's key, can't show the notification arriving; the mention node is the evidence that Linear notifies the mentioned user.
- 2026-11-19, ticket 20 (m3): both rows are `partial`. Fan-out and delivery are proved for the driver and the project's members, on the panel, the ticket and email (ticket 21: a due mail is sent once, skipped when read or closed), against a real database and queue: one notification per member and one delivery per wanted channel, a replay sends nothing twice, a member who can't open the project and another organization's members get nothing, a member's off switch stops only their channel, the ticket's comment is edited inside the window and posted after it, a failing tracker leaves the other channels delivered and ends `failed` in words, and every writer of a needs-you item enqueues (`needs-you-writers.test.ts` reads the whole repo). The task's reviewers and the owners of a refine draft are not audiences yet (milestones 5 and later), and mail is ticket 21's.
- 2026-11-20: the panel's list counted open notifications while Home counted open items, so a task stopped with its no-changes item open showed on Home and not on the page. The page's Open tab and its count, and the sidebar's count (`notifications.openCount`, which replaces `unreadCount`), now read Home's rule, and the member's notification only says whether they read the row and which of their deliveries failed. Stopping a task, a move that ends it and core's clean-up dismiss its open items with their fan-out, so their notifications close too. Row 2 gains those tests and stays `partial`.
- 2026-11-20, m6 ticket 04: `NotificationAdapter`'s moments gain `draft_ready`, `review_requested`, `task_approval`, `round_cap` and `automation_paused` (`teamNotificationKinds`), and the notification contract suite checks each reaches every adapter's channel with the task's title, key and one line (in the email's HTML and text), and that neither the title nor the line reaches a log line. It runs on the panel, ticket, Resend and SMTP adapters; being in a suite file, it's not cited. Break-its on the SMTP adapter: logging the subject failed all five; sending an empty text part failed all five. Who gets them is core's (tickets 15, 17 to 19, 14, 10).
- 2026-11-20: a task that ended three turns without changes had three open `no_changes` items, and Home listed three rows keyed by the one task (React: "two children with the same key"). A new item of the same reason now supersedes the task's open one (`supersedesOpen` in `needs-you-items.ts`): the older is dismissed through `resolveNeedsYouItemsInTx`, so its fan-out closes its notifications, and the newer is the moment with its own. Updating the older in place was rejected: fan-out is keyed by the item and the change, so a second moment would notify nobody, and Home's newest-first order would keep the old time. Approvals, questions and plans stack, each on its own request. Core's clean-up collapses the duplicates older releases left. Row 2 gains those tests and stays `partial`.
- 2026-11-20, m6 ticket 19: the task's reviewers are an audience. A code task waiting for a reviewer has one `waiting_for_reviewer` item (audience `reviewer`, the moment `review_requested`), which reaches its matched reviewers who can open the project on the panel, the ticket and email, each by their own choices, and the driver when nobody is matched (`resolveAudience` with the task's reviewers from `TaskReviewer`). A reviewer whose review comes in has their own notification answered; the item is answered once every matched reviewer reviewed. Both rows stay `partial`: the refine draft's owners are ticket 15's.
- 2026-11-20, ticket 44 (m3): the panel's own e2e case: an approval reaches a member who isn't its driver on Home and Needs you, is answered there and moves to Done (`21-needs-you.spec.ts`).
