## Notes

- **The first condition is proved** (milestone 7, tickets 36 and 13). The domain's registry (`paletteActions`, ticket 36) is the one list: the panel's palette (`apps/web/src/components/search-palette.tsx` with `components/shell/actions/`), its key listener (`use-shortcuts.ts`) and the `?` sheet (`shortcut-sheet.tsx`) all read it. In Playwright on the production build, each of "jump to needs you", approve, answer, take over and reopen runs from the palette with the keyboard only and from its shortcut, end to end on seeded tasks (`e2e/seed-keys.ts`), each checked in the database (`e2e/read-keys.ts`); the sheet's lines are compared with the registry, and every line is pressed and its effect checked (a line with no check fails the test); `s` asks to confirm; no shortcut fires while typing in the composer or the palette.
- **The second condition is proved** (tickets 16 and 17). Ticket 16: approve, answer, take over and reopen done with Tab, arrows and Enter alone on their own screens (the approval card takes the focus when it appears and its buttons follow in order; the Needs you list is one Tab stop with the arrows between rows; the question's form; the take-over bar; the reopen composer, where Enter sends and Shift+Enter is a new line); the focus goes back to its trigger after the palette, the sheet, Stop's confirmation and a menu; a polite live region announces a new needs-you item. Ticket 17 (`apps/web/e2e/a11y/`, ostra's own code on Playwright, no new dependency, Q261): on the production build, every route of `src/app` (the list `e2e/routes.ts` keeps, which the first test checks against the route tree), and the task page waiting on an approval, a question and a spend cap, on seeded data (`e2e/seed-routes-world.ts`), in the dark and the light theme (chosen through `account.setTheme`; a page drawn in the other theme fails), at 1440 px (`--project a11y-desktop`) and 390 px (`--project a11y-phone`): the keyboard walk (Tab from the top reaches every visible control once, in document order, each with a 2px outline or ring at 3:1 against what's behind it, and comes back round with no trap) and the contrast check (every visible text node at 4.5:1, or 3:1 when large; placeholders; the edges of fields and of bordered buttons at 3:1); and on every dialog, popover, menu and select a route opens from a trigger that says so (`aria-haspopup`, a select's combobox), opened with Enter, plus the ⌘K palette, its Approve… step and the `?` sheet: the same checks inside it, the focus kept inside a dialog, the arrows through a menu's items, Escape closing it (the palette's step goes back a step first) and the focus back on its trigger. Disabled controls, and text drawn over a picture (the sign-in illustration's tagline, where no colour can be computed), are listed in the test's attachment, not failed. The run: `pnpm --filter @ostra/web exec playwright test --project a11y-desktop --project a11y-phone` (6 passed, 11 minutes), by hand like the rest of the e2e suite.
