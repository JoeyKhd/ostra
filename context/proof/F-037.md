## Notes

- 2026-11-19, ticket 04 (m4): the first row is `partial`. What is proved is the adapter contract: a models.dev entry carries all four prices per million tokens (fresh input, output, cache read, cache write), each `null` where the catalog has none, and `matchCatalog` carries them unchanged (the model provider contract suite, on the tiny adapter and the model endpoint adapter). Spend at ingest, the rollups and the screens are tickets 12, 27 and 36.
- **The gate row is partial** (milestone 4's ticket 06): `budgetGate` over every origin as pure code. Tickets 12 and 13 call it at every turn start and hold and release the turns.
- 2026-11-19, ticket 12 (m4): the gate row is `proved`. `checkBudget` runs in the task's lock at the dispatch, a reopen (and every rendered action that reopens), the composer's message and every rendered message to an idle session; its table test holds every origin, `automatic-round` and `closing-turn` included, whose callers milestone 6 and ticket 16 add. The spend row stays `partial`: the rollup is proved, the screens are tickets 27 and 36.
- 2026-11-19, ticket 13 (m4): `budget.check` writes one `BudgetNotice` per budget, month and threshold and fans it out, in the same transaction, to the organization's admins and, for a project budget, that project's admins: a panel delivery and a mail each, the mail on by default and off for a member who turned it off. `setBudget` (the domain use case; its router is ticket 27) checks the month at once and enqueues `budget.release` on a raise above the spend; `budget.new-month` (00:05 UTC on the 1st) enqueues one per budget. The release runs each held turn through the gate again, oldest first, and re-offers the tasks waiting at it. The budget row and the 80% row stay `partial`: the router and the Spend page are tickets 27 and 36, and the panel's notifications list (`notifications.list`) shows only rows with a needs-you item, so the budget notices it now delivers show on the page with ticket 36.
- 2026-11-19, ticket 27 (m4): the API. `spend.month` and `spend.project` read `SpendDaily` only, fold a project the member can't open into "Other projects" (its budget isn't listed for them), and list spend per model and per provider, the unpriced tokens with their models, and each budget with its share, state and overshoot. `budgets.set` and `budgets.clear` (organization admins, ADR 0055) call ticket 13's `setBudget`. `providers.setModelPrice` writes the four prices with `priceSource: "admin"`, and a catalog refresh now keeps an admin's price. `tasks.startCheck` gives the New task page the dispatch's own reason. The budget row is `proved` through the API; the spend row stays `partial` until the Spend page (ticket 36).
- 2026-11-20, ticket 36 (m4): the panel. Spend (`/organization/spend`) reads `spend.month`, with the budget and price forms; Models shows a model's four prices and their source with `Edit price`; the project page shows its month; New task and the Inbox give the gate's reason before Start (the task still starts, queued); the task page words a task queued by the budget, a held message (`Held`) and the thread's held and released rows; Home and Needs you list the member's budget notices this month (`notifications.budgetNotices`, new). The Playwright case runs core in its stack, so the dispatch, the gate and the release are core's own; no runner is connected, so "starts both" is shown as the held turn released (its steer handed to the seeded runner's outbox) and the waiting task offered again, now waiting only for a runner. The spend, 80% and 100% rows are `proved`; the per-member and per-profile row stays `missing` (F-131, later).
- 2026-11-20, OpenCode's subagents (`context/research/opencode-subagents.md`): a subagent runs in the same OpenCode process and calls the model through the task's one provider, the key proxy, so each of its calls is counted like the main agent's, also after the main agent's turn has ended; OpenCode's own turn figures (the prompt's `usage`, `usage_update`'s `cost`) leave a subagent's calls out, and ostra doesn't read them for spend.
