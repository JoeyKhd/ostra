## Notes

- **Ticket 11 (2026-11-20)** built the bulk start (`packages/domain/src/starts/bulk.ts`, core's `bulk.start-item` worker): one `BulkStart` and its items in one transaction, idempotent by the member's request key, at most `OSTRA_BULK_START_MAX` (50), and each item through the one start (`startTask`) in its own transaction, with an outcome per ticket (`started`, `skipped` with `has-task`, `no-access`, `not-on-a-board` or `has-sub-issues`, or `failed` with the refusal's code). Every task it starts goes through the real dispatch's gates and waits queued with the reason (`apps/core/src/testing/start-gates.ts`).
- The first row stays `partial` until the Inbox's selection and the dry run's "Start all" button reach `startMany` (tickets 25 and 32); the use case and its worker are proved here.
- A dry run's "Start all" starts what the rule would: the rule's kind, its profile unless the member chose one, and its pool labels; each task keeps the rule's ID (`automationId`) and the member as its starter (`origin: member`).
