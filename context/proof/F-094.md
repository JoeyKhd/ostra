Notes:
- The `host` half of the fifth row, the executor refusing a spec with services with the reason ostra shows and making no box, is milestone 3's ticket 15 (`host-cache-and-checks.test.ts`); the executor contract suite's case "F-094: an executor without services refuses a spec that has any, and makes no box" runs the same refusal against every executor.
- Also proved for ticket 27, beyond the "done when": a service's secret reaches the runner in the credentials only ("F-094: the spec carries the service with its plain variables only; its secret is in the credentials and nowhere else"), and a service that fails to start fails the setup ("F-094: a service that fails to start fails the setup, with its name and reason, as a failed setup does"), both in `apps/core/src/dispatch/services.test.ts`.
- Ticket 40, 2026-11-19: the API half of the first and third rows, through `environments.services.*` and `environments.get`. A service's secret is stored by reference and read back as `set`; its health is the last `service.healthy` or `service.failed` of the project's boxes, with the runner and the time. The editor and the health on the page are ticket 48; the agent reaching a service by name is the `docker` executor's (ticket 14), so both rows stay `partial`.
- Ticket 48, 2026-11-19: the panel. The environment's `Services and checks` tab saves a service (image, port, a secret that reads back as `set` and never reaches the page, a health check) and lists it; with only a `host` runner in the project's pools the page says the project's tasks wait, and a task waiting with `needs-services-runner` names the runners online on its page (`apps/web/e2e/08-environment-services.spec.ts`, desktop and phone). The first row is `proved`; the third stays `partial` until the `docker` executor's live run (ticket 14) shows the agent reaching a service by name.
