## Notes

- **Row 1** (tickets 06, 28, 37): the six figures' reference definitions over rows, `insights.get` over the job's rows, and the page for the organization and a project over 7, 30 or 90 days or two dates, with the period in the URL. The job that fills `InsightDaily` is row 2, ticket 14's.
- **Ticket 28 (2026-11-19):** `insights.get` reads only `InsightDaily`, `TaskMetric`, `InsightRun` and `SpendDaily`. Row 3 stays partial until ticket 14's job is the only writer of those rows. `InsightDaily.waitingByReason` holds each ended item's duration by reason (`insightWaitingByReasonSchema`, `waitingDurationsByReason` in `packages/domain/src/insights/figures.ts`), so the median per item is exact over any period: ticket 14 writes it with that function.
- 2026-11-19, ticket 14 (m4): `insights.compute` runs every `OSTRA_INSIGHTS_INTERVAL_MINUTES` (`apps/core/src/reactions/insights.ts`, `packages/domain/src/use-cases/insights.ts`). Per organization, under an advisory lock, it recomputes today, yesterday and every day a changed PR, an ended needs-you item or a new task touches since `InsightRun.lastRunAt`, and the touched tasks' `TaskMetric`, from ticket 06's definitions; it reads only `TaskPullRequest`, `NeedsYouItem` and `Task` (its deps are the database and a clock, so no adapter can be called), and writes a row only when its facts changed. Row 2 is `proved`. Row 3 is now `proved` too: the job is the only writer of the rows `insights.get` reads. Row 1 waited for the page (ticket 37).
- 2026-11-19, ticket 37 (m4): the page for the organization and each project reads `insights.get` for the period in the URL (7, 30 or 90 days, or two dates); `apps/web/e2e/09-insights.spec.ts` checks its figures against `insights.get`'s. Row 1 is `proved`.
- 2026-11-20, ticket 41 (m4): the live demo showed "PRs merged 0" after two merges. A webhook or the reconcile moved the task repo to merged, but never the `TaskPullRequest` row the job reads. `recordPullRequestState` now moves that row too (merged or closed with its time, open again on a reopen).
