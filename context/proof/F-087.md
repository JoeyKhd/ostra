## Notes

- 2026-11-19, ticket 09 (m4): the restricted project holds a row of every project model; a member not listed on it reads none of their IDs through any query procedure, the live stream or search, and is refused with NOT_FOUND (the matrix).
- 2026-11-19, ticket 23 (m4): `projects.setAccess`, `projects.setMember` and `projects.removeMember`, each an audit entry, and the live stream looking again at once on a member's or a project's notice. *A project admin edits* stays `partial` for the boards, automations and memory, which are their tickets' to prove.
- 2026-11-19, ticket 33 (m4): a project's Access tab in its settings, read-only for its members. Restricting a project now reaches an unlisted member who had it open at once: the stream sends them that project's next change once, the one notice about a project they could see, and nothing of it after (`packages/api/src/stream/subscribe.ts`).
- 2026-11-20, ticket 41 (m4): the write side of the matrix, `packages/api/src/isolation/every-write.test.ts`. Every write of the members, the connections (GitHub and Linear), the model providers, the runner pools, the agent profiles, the budgets and `projects.create` refuses an organization member and a project admin, and lets the organization admin through, with a guard on any new write of those routers; the admin's real calls in each area go through. A project admin edits the repos, a board, the environment, an automation, the memory and the default profile of its project, and a project member is refused each. A project member who is no admin starts, steers, takes over, stops and reopens a task, and every member who can open the project reads it and its conversation. Proved with fakes for GitHub, Linear and the model endpoint only.
