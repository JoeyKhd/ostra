## Comments

**2026-11-23, built (web)** as `✨ put a turn's numbers in its footer and panel` (its hash is in the next entry). What it does, and the choices made:

- **The footer** (`thread/turn-numbers.tsx`, `RunFooter`): `footerFacts` in mono 12px `--faint`, each fact and the ` ·` after it in one unbreakable span, so the row wraps only between facts (ticket 10's note); `Event log ›` at its right to `?tab=events&seq={first seq}`, on a line of its own at phone width (`s6-phone-open`). The clamp's fact keeps its sentence as its `title`.
- **The time's tooltip** (`TurnTime`, `TurnTip`): the time is a `tabindex="0"` span with the dotted `--line-3` underline on hover and focus. Its tooltip shows after 300 ms of hover and at once on focus, stays while the pointer crosses onto it (150 ms) or the focus stays, and Escape closes it wherever the focus is (WCAG 1.4.13). 268px on `--raise` with `--shadow-pop`, its left edge 10px left of the time, 13px above it, flipping below when the thread's scroll box leaves no room. It's in the page only while it shows (`aria-describedby` then), so a turn's numbers are never the column's text, even hidden. The timing is a pure `createTipControl` over injected timers, tested with fake ones. `tooltipRows` now gives each row its `figure`, set in mono 12px `--text-2` (all of `22.4k in · 3.8k out`, the `193 kB` of `archive 193 kB`).
