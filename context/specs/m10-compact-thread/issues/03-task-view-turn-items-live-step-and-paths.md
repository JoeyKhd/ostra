## What to build

Pure functions over one `TaskUIMessage`, so the panel renders items and never groups parts itself, and every test and viewer sees the same grouping.

- **`turnItemsOf(message, { taskId, checkoutDirs, mcpServers })`** → `TurnItem[]` as the spec's section 5.3 lists: `run`, `prose`, `captures`, `failure`, `notice`, `result`, `needs`, in the turn's order. A result or an open needs-you part ends the run before it; prose closes a run; the turn's first run is `firstInTurn` (the line that carries the time).
- **`RunStep`** for every part a run holds, discriminated by kind with exhaustive `switch`es: every read of a run as one step with its distinct paths; an edit's lines added and removed from its diff; a command's line, exit (`killed` as its own value) and duration from `meta.stepTimes`; a thought's first sentence (120 characters at most); a search's query, folder and matches; a fetch's host, path and status; a capture with its route from the attachment's `target.path` (never `/` by default) and its preview's label and port; a subagent's node; the setup script; the PR's text; ostra's own tools by `ostraToolOf`, with their outcome (a repeat review's `already submitted, kept as it was`); any other tool by its name and its MCP server (a title starting with one of `mcpServers` and `_`), never the kind `other`.
- **Counts and the tail:** a run's counts by kind in the order each kind's first step happened; its total; its recovered and unrecovered failures; its approvals' answers (approved by whom, denied by whom and whether with a reason); its subagents' counts.
- **Recovered** (`recovered.ts`): a failed step is recovered when a later step in the same turn, of the same kind and the same subject (command line, path, URL), succeeded; a cancelled step isn't a failure. An unrecovered failure is also its own `failure` item right after its run. A failed turn, a crashed session and a lost box are `failure` items; a limit, a budget hold and an approval that expired are `notice` items.
- **`liveStepOf(message)`:** what the agent does now from the running part, the turn's finished steps, its failures not yet recovered, the plan step in progress (`data-plan`'s `in_progress` entry, its index and the plan's length), the turn's start; a pending approval's step as `waiting`, which stands in the live step's place, with the approval's block as a `needs` item after it.
- **`workspacePath(raw, context)`, `workspaceCommand(line, context)`:** the spec's section 5.3 rule for `host` (`…/tasks/{taskId}/agent/`, either separator), `docker` and `kubernetes` (`/workspace/`), a one-repo task's checkout folder, and the home folder as `~/`; a step keeps its full value for `title`.
- Exported from the package's index for the panel; no React, no words (ticket 04 words them).

The two catches the ticket names were checked once: matching a later success of any subject fails the `pnpm test a` case, and taking `\` out of the `host` folder's separators fails the Windows cases. Decisions and readings:
  - **`steps.ts`** also builds a message's pieces (`piecesOf`): a step, prose, a question, captures ostra took after the turn, a result, a preview shown, a row's failure or notice. `items.ts` groups them, `live-step.ts` reads them, and `recovered.ts` holds the rule. A turn's atoms are one step per call, thought or capture. A run's `steps` then put every read on one step, at the first read's place (`toolCallIds` lists the calls, `fullSubject` joins the full paths).
  - **Names beside the spec's** (the spec's 5.3 has a dated note): a step's full value is `fullSubject`, since the PR's text step keeps `title` for the PR's title. A failed step's own error words are its `error` (data, never logged), and a `failure` item is `{ cause: "step", step }`. Ticket 04 builds its words from the step (`exit 2 after 38 s`, `403 Forbidden`, from `exit`, `ms` and a fetch's `status: { code, text }`).
  - **`open` on a run:** the turn runs and nothing it said or asked follows the run. The live step takes that line's place (ticket 11). Its failures get no red line while a later step may still recover them: the live step counts them (`1 failed`). Once prose closes the run, an unrecovered failure is its own `failure` item right after it. A turn whose last item is prose has no open run: the live step goes after the prose, before the results.
  - **Recovered** is read over the whole turn, so a later run's success recovers an earlier run's failure. The subjects: the raw command line, the read's path, an edit's paths, a fetch's URL, a search's query and folder, a web search's query, a tool's title, ostra's tool kind. A cancelled step (the turn's cancel), a denied one and one whose approval closed are `cancelled`, never failures; the receipt on the step says which.
  - **The order of a run's own items:** the run, its unrecovered failures, an expired approval's notice, then its captures (milestone 8's strip, one item per `data-attachments` part).
  - **A question** is a `needs` item whether it's open or not (`open: false` once answered or closed): its receipt stands in the block's place. It ends the run before it either way, so the steps after an answer are a new run (S2's turn 1). **A pending approval** leaves its run, which closes, for a `needs` item with its step. Answered, the step is back in its run with its receipt, and counts in the tail (`approved` by whom, `denied` by whom and `withReason`). An approval that expired adds a `notice` after its run.
  - **The plan** isn't a turn's `needs` item: a turn's parts hold no proposal (`plan.proposed` draws no part since milestone 3; the block is `tasks.get`'s `plan`, as `task-view.tsx` draws it today). Ticket 13 places it after its turn's items. The turn's `data-plan` is no item either: the live step reads its entry in progress.
  - **Counts** are in the order each kind's first step happened. Reads and edits count distinct files, captures their pictures, the rest their steps. `web-search` and `fetch` stay apart, and ostra's own tools and the PR's text count as `tool`. `total` is every call once (a thought and an info line aren't steps). So S3's turn 2 counts `read 9, web-search 3, fetch 1, command 8, tool 2` in that order, where its target's hand-written line has `ran 8 commands, searched the web 4 times`; S3's turn 1 counts its hand-in as `tool 1`, which its target's line leaves out. Ticket 04 chooses the words from these counts (whether to merge web searches and fetches, and whether ostra's hand-in is on the line).
  - **Results** come after the turn's last slot, in order. The PR's `pr` item goes right after the push whose `firstPr` it is (its card), once. A preview shown (`show_preview`, done) is a `preview` result as well as its `ostra` step; its label, port and path are its input's, read leniently (`null` where the agent named none). `data-pr-text` is no item: its text is the PR card's title, and the agent's `set_pull_request_text` call is the `pr-text` step, matched by its title with any agent's prefix, as `ostraToolOf` reads ostra's other tools (the protocol's `ostraToolOf` stays as ticket 01 made it).
  - **ostra's steps:** `ostraToolOf` names them. A `submit_review` that's done takes `outcome: "already-in"` when its turn's matching `data-review` (in order) is a repeat, else `"done"`. A screenshot call whose picture came stays folded into its capture (milestone 8), so a capture step's route is the attachment's own `path`, and its `preview` the attachment's `target` (a process or a service as `label`, a port as `port`).
  - **A generic tool's** title splits into `server` and `name` when it starts with one of `mcpServers` and `_` (the longest server wins); a `dynamic-tool`'s kind word reaches no item. A web search is a generic tool whose name, lowercased and letters only, ends in `websearch` (OpenCode's `websearch`, `web_search`, `brave_web_search`). A subagent step is a call its tree names in `startedBy` or a round's `toolCallId`; its run's `subagents` count that node and the ones nested under it.
  - **Rows outside a turn:** a crashed session and a lost box are `failure` items; a limit and a budget hold are `notice` items (a round's hold too, which ticket 12 draws in its round's bubble); the setup script is a run of its one step (`firstInTurn: false`); a member's capture from the pane is a `captures` item. A member's message and the dividers (a restore, a new driver, an effort change, a stop) have no item: ticket 12 draws them from their message.
  - **`liveStepOf(message, context)`** takes the context, since paths and tool names need it; the spec's sketch had `now`, but the clock is the panel's, from `startedAt`. `doing` is the latest running step or the prose streaming, and `thinking` when nothing runs; several reads at once are `reading` with a `count`. It's `null` when the turn ended or failed, and while the turn waits on its open question with nothing running (S1). A pending approval's step is `waiting`, beside whatever else runs. `done` counts every settled call (failed and cancelled ones too, a capture each picture), so S1-working reads 8.
  - **Two additive fields in the fold** (`fold.ts`, `ui-types.ts`), so the rows match their targets. `StepTime.answeredAt` is when an approval let its call run (a member's or a rule's answer, never before the call's start), so `pnpm db:migrate` reads 2.3 s, not the 70.9 s it waited. The answered receipt's `withReason: true` says a deny gave a reason, never its words. `CANCELLED_TOOL_ERROR` names the cancelled call's error text, which the fold writes and `steps.ts` reads.
  - **Paths:** a step's paths are written with `/` once a prefix is taken off; a command line keeps the agent's own separators. A quoted `host` path may hold spaces; an unquoted one ends at the shell's boundaries. The repo's folder alone reads `.`. Another task's `host` folder isn't this task's workspace: it only reads as under the home folder.
  - **For later tickets:** the panel passes `{ taskId, checkoutDirs, mcpServers }`. `checkoutDirs` are the repos' folders in the workspace (their names), and `mcpServers` the names the agent's MCP tools start with. `tasks.get` gives neither yet: ticket 09 wires them, and an empty list reads every title whole. Ticket 10 draws a run's `steps` (a read step's `paths`, an edit's `files` with their `patch` for `diff ›`, a command's `output`, a thought's `text` for `show ›`, a tool's `input` for `input ›`) and its failure and notice items; ticket 11 the open run's live step; ticket 13 the `needs` items; ticket 14 the results.
