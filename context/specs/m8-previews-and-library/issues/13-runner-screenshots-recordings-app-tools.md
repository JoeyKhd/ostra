## What to build

- **The four tools** on ostra's MCP server in the sidecar, for a task whose spec has `"app"`, with the schemas and result texts of ticket 01:
  - `start_app`: ticket 12's start, which returns each process's state, address and log path.
  - `show_preview`: ticket 12's announce.
  - `take_screenshot`: a page of its own in the box's browser at the size asked; `http://localhost:<port><path>` (or a service's address); a wait for the network to go quiet, at most 15 s; a PNG within the limits, full page when asked and capped at 10,000 px; uploaded on the bulk path with the purpose `app-screenshot`; then `attachment.added` with the source `agent`. The result names the attachment's number in the task.
  - `record_app`: its steps in a page of its own, each step with a deadline, under a screencast at 10 frames a second; the frames, their times and the steps in one frame-reel blob (`app-recording`) within the limits; then `attachment.added`. A step that fails ends the recording there, keeps what it has, and says which step failed.
- **The frame reel** is written in ticket 01's format (`packages/protocol`, `src/task/frame-reel.ts`), which the panel's player reads (ticket 17).
- **After a turn.** After the commit, the push and the hidden ref (ADR 0039), if the turn changed a repo, the environment has a primary and `screenshots.afterTurn` is on: start what isn't running, wait for the primary, and take each route at each width, with the source `after-turn`. A primary that doesn't get ready sends `app.process failed` and takes nothing. The turn's end is never held past the ready check's 120 s.
- **A member's screenshot.** `screenshot.take` from the pane takes one of the preview's current path, with the source `member` and the member's ID.
- **Limits.** 20 attachments a turn; past that, the tool's result says so, and the after-turn screenshots are skipped with a note in the thread.
- **What's recorded.** Each attachment's event names the browser's name and version. The caption and the steps go in the event and the blob, never in a log line.

One picture at a time, each in a page of its own, closed after. The page-level work is `browser/pictures.ts`.
  - **`take_screenshot` and `record_app`** on the sidecar's MCP server for the `app` set. Refused first, in the protocol's words: a port below 1024 or one of the box's own (`AppProcesses.refusePort`, the rule `show_preview` uses), a process the environment lacks, the turn's 20 pictures; then no browser (`appToolRefusalText` with `noBrowserWords`); then a process target is started with those before it in the list (by the list's convention its API comes first) and must get ready, or `not-ready` names its log. A port target is opened as it is. The answer names the attachment's ID, `att-` and 12 random characters, unique across a task's boxes: what "the attachment's number" became, and what a finding names.
  - **A screenshot** loads `http://localhost:<port><path>` at the preset (desktop 1440×900; tablet and phone as touch devices) or the size, waits for the network to go quiet (no request in flight for 500 ms, from the Network domain's events, an EventSource never counted; at most 15 s, then it's taken as it is), and captures a PNG: the viewport, or the full page capped at 10,000 px high and 3,840 wide. On a phone or a tablet a page laid out wider than the screen (no viewport tag: 980 px) is zoomed out to fit, as the phone shows it (`page.ts`'s `fitWidth`). Over 8 MiB it isn't kept, and the answer says to take the viewport or a smaller size.
  - **A recording** loads its first page before the screencast (the first step's `goto`, recorded at 0 ms, or `/`), so the reel opens on the app; then JPEG q60 at most 1440×900, `maxFramesInFlight` 1, each ack held to 10 frames a second (spike 07). Each step has a 10 s deadline (`RECORD_STEP_DEADLINE_MS`) within the recording's length (`seconds`, at most 30). A step that fails ends it: the reel keeps the frames and the steps that ran, the failed one included, and the answer says which step and why (`recordStepFailureText`); a first `goto` that fails records nothing. Frames past 300, or past 32 MiB less 512 KiB of header room, aren't kept; a page that sent no frame gets one still JPEG. `record_app` takes no width, so it's the desktop preset.

Nothing of a caption, a step, a path or an address is logged: IDs, kinds, sources, sizes and counts only.
