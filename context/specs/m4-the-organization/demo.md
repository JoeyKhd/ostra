# Milestone 4 demo: the organization

Ticket 41 runs spec section 3 and records it here. This is the scripted run (`pnpm e2e:m4`, `e2e/m4-the-organization/`), live on the `host` executor against the test workspaces. The manual run in three real browsers, with Resend and the reviewer user, is still the maintainer's (see "What's missing").

## The run, 2026-11-20

**Where.** A worktree of `origin/main` (`ow/m4-integrate`), `pnpm install`, an empty data folder (`.ostra-dev` and the runners' folders deleted), and a fresh dev stack on ports 3830, 9430 and 55030 with `OSTRA_ALLOW_DEFAULT_PASSWORD=true`, `OSTRA_INSIGHTS_INTERVAL_MINUTES=1`, `OSTRA_BOX_IDLE_MINUTES=2` and `OSTRA_RECONCILE_MINUTES=2`. Windows 11, Node 24. Two personal runners on `host` in processes of their own: Demo's `m4-runner`, and Sam's `m4-runner-2`, which Sam approved once Sam had joined. Core was reached through a Cloudflare quick tunnel, for Linear's webhook. Mail went over SMTP to ticket 40's sink (`pnpm dev:mail-sink`), and the script read the invitation, verification and reset links from it.

**Who.** The panel was driven with Playwright as three people, each in a browser context of their own: Demo (the default first admin, `demo@demo.com`), Sam (`sam@demo.test`) and Rae (`rae@demo.test`), both of whom arrived by invitation.

**What it ran on.**
- Linear: the `ostra-test` workspace, team `OST` (OST-191 for step 4, OST-192 for step 8; both archived by the clean-up).
- GitHub: the org `ostra-test`, repos `test1` and `test2`, through the test App `ostra-sandbox-036d7c`.
- The model: `model.env`'s endpoint (DeepSeek V4.1 Flash on OpenRouter), as provider `client-a`. The script priced it at $20 input, $60 output, $2 cache read and $20 cache write per million tokens, so that a task costs whole cents and a budget can be crossed. With no second key on this machine (ticket 55), `client-b` is a second provider on the same endpoint and key: the routing per project is real, and the separation of the tokens is ticket 48's tests.

**How it went.**
- **Run 6, from the empty data folder (13:08 to 14:22 UTC):** 133 of 143 checks passed. Two earlier runs that day (runs 4 and 5) found the product bugs below, and were stopped once they had.
- **Re-runs of the failed steps,** on the same stack and data (14:25 to 14:55 UTC), after fixing the script's reads: search, removal, step 15, step 16, the checks after the run, and the clean-up.
- **Every failed check is accounted for below.** The script's own state, every check with its detail, is in [`demo/checks.json`](demo/checks.json): six invocations, 205 checks. The screenshots are in [`demo/`](demo/).

### What the run found, and fixed

The product fixes have their tests and break-its, and their proof rows.
- **Invitation IDs and verification tokens in the dev log** (`dd90184b`): `next dev` logged each request's URL. `apps/web/next.config.ts` now leaves out `/invite/…`, `/reset…`, `?token=`, `?verify=`, the invitation and reset reads and `/api/auth/` (`logging.incomingRequests.ignore`).
- **`repo:` in search took only one project's row of a repo** (`1bb78bf9`, F-080): a repo two projects list has a row in each, so `repo:test2` missed tasks in the other project. It now names every row of that repo.
